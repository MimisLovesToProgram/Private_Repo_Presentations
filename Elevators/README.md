# 🛗 Elevator Professionals Convenience App

## 💡 The Idea

Besides standard note-taking and generic software, elevator-repair professionals have few available options when it comes to storing & organizing data regarding their current customers and their elevators, with all the required info like maintenances, repairs and general data.

Handling such information neatly can be very difficult when professionals have to **juggle through pages** of notes about many elevators, their characteristics & where they left off in each one of them, without any clear structure, order and a standard note-taking procedure. 

## 🚀 An Interesting Project-Solution?

A low-cost app for elevator professionals to manage their current jobs, access & change their data from any platform or device - and most importantly,
**smoothen out their workflow and make the whole information storage & retrieval process trivial and intuitive.**

The elevator data is stored in a **central database** so it can easily be accessed from anywhere.
The app is written in Flutter with Dart, and communicates with the backend server & database via an API I developed using Flask & Python.

The much-simplified workflow is as follows:
1. The professional logs in the app. They have the option to pick "Remember Me" so they can skip this step later and save time.
2. If they are close to the location of one of the elevators they're working on, they are asked "Are you working there now?", and if so, they are quickly directed to the elevator's details.
3. Otherwise, they can choose to either add a new elevator they're working on, or access & change the details of another existing elevator.
4. The elevator addition process is straightforward - they can either type an address and pick the appropriate location from a map, or click a point on the map to quickly get it's address and save the new elevator.
5. If they choose to edit the information of an elevator, the fields & checkboxes they may need are nicely positioned and structured, while otherwise-untidy pieces of data like maintenances and repairs lists are conveniently sorted.
